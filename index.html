THE GAME

Pickaxes, contracts and stocks

You play as a young noble lady whose family has lost everything. Mine silver with a pickaxe, sign contracts to summon maids, and send them into battle. While your maids fight, the stock market keeps moving: buy if you think prices will rise, or go short if you think they will fall.

Mine silver

Swing your pickaxe and keep the combo going. Not every swing pays off, but honest work keeps the lights on.

Summon maids

Spend silver on maid contracts and stack them to send more maids into battle. 36 maids across 12 roles, each with her own fighting style.

Trade stocks

Buy, or go short. 13 stocks react to the battle in real time. A good trade can turn a losing fight around.
