Shinomiya Rin. Seventeen years old.

Former noble lady. Current occupation: miner.

Her father called himself a "genius of investment". Then he put the family fortune into whatever the crowd at the exchange swore would go up. Three times. All three crashed.

The creditors took the manor and the land. All that's left for Rin is a crumbling old house and an abandoned mine her father bought with his last coins. And then, one angry swing of the pickaxe cracks the wall open: silver.

Now she has a plan. Mine the silver, hire maids, play the market, and bring the family back.

"...Of everything Father bought, the first useful thing has finally turned up."— Rin, holding her pickaxe

Pickaxes, contracts and stocks

A 1v1 auto-battle strategy game. Your maids fight on their own, so you can focus on the money.

Mine silver

Swing your pickaxe and keep the combo going. Not every swing pays off, but honest work keeps the lights on.

Summon maids

Spend silver on maid contracts and stack them to send more maids into battle. Who you send together matters.

Trade stocks

Buy, or go short. 13 stocks react to the battle in real time. A good trade can turn a losing fight around.

The household

Four companions to come home to. The one you bring decides which skill you can use in battle.

36 maids, 12 jobs

Some hold the front line, some heal from the back, and some just explode. Every maid is earned by playing.
